How it works (Overdraft Logic): When Minus Booking is enabled for a portal, the user can create bookings even with ₹0 or insufficient wallet balance. The wallet balance may drop into negative numbers up to the assigned Overdraft Limit (Target Amount). Once the negative balance reaches the limit, further bookings will be restricted until the wallet is recharged, with all transactions recorded in the financial ledger.
